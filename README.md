# 60 WPM Typing Test

**Designed by imacKrisK** | Contact: imKrisK@icloud.com

A professional typing test application designed to help users achieve 60 words per minute typing speed with real-time feedback and progress tracking.

## 🎯 Features

- **Real-time WPM tracking** - See your speed as you type
- **Accuracy monitoring** - Track your typing precision
- **60-second timer** - Standard typing test duration
- **Progress tracking** - Characters typed vs. total text
- **Live visual feedback** - Color-coded correct/incorrect typing
- **Multiple difficulty levels** - Easy, Medium, and Hard texts
- **Achievement system** - Goal-based feedback and encouragement
- **Responsive design** - Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Open `typing-test.html` in any web browser
2. Choose your difficulty level (Easy, Medium, or Hard)
3. Click "Start Test" to begin the 60-second challenge
4. Type the displayed text in the input area
5. Watch your WPM and accuracy update in real-time
6. Review your results and try to beat your score!

## 🎮 Controls

- **Start Test** - Begin the typing challenge
- **Reset** - Clear current progress and start over
- **New Text** - Generate a new text sample
- **Escape** - Quick reset (keyboard shortcut)
- **F5** - Generate new text (keyboard shortcut)

## 🏆 Achievement Goals

- **🎉 Excellent**: 60+ WPM with 95%+ accuracy
- **👍 Good**: 40+ WPM with 90%+ accuracy  
- **💪 Keep Practicing**: Below 40 WPM or 90% accuracy

## 💡 Tips to Reach 60 WPM

- **🏠 Home Row Position**: Keep fingers on ASDF and JKL;
- **👀 Don't Look Down**: Train yourself to type without looking at keys
- **⚡ Accuracy First**: Speed comes naturally with accurate typing
- **🔄 Practice Daily**: 15-20 minutes of consistent practice
- **📝 Focus on Rhythm**: Develop a steady typing rhythm
